Personal project, 2026

mcquant: a Monte Carlo engine that checks its own answers

A Python library that prices options, measures portfolio risk and values companies by simulation. Every Monte Carlo price in the test suite is compared against an independent exact formula, binomial tree or characteristic-function integral, and must land within four standard errors.

  • 33,700× variance reduction on an arithmetic Asian call by combining randomised Sobol quasi-Monte Carlo with a control variate.
  • 13 of 13 validation benchmarks pass, backed by 299 tests at 98% coverage and CI on GitHub Actions.
  • A full valuation of Monde Nissin (PSE: MONDE) from public filings: DCF, 50,000-run Monte Carlo DCF, trading comps and a live Excel model.

Python, NumPy, SciPy, pandas, Streamlit, pytest, Hypothesis, GitHub Actions

Personal project, 2026

Regime-switching portfolio model

A two-state Hidden Markov Model reads calm and turbulent markets from 16 years of price data, and the portfolio switches between a maximum-Sharpe allocation and a minimum-volatility one with equity capped at 30%. Features are lagged a day to avoid look-ahead bias, and the model is retrained every month in a walk-forward backtest.

  • Halved maximum drawdown against a 60/40 benchmark over 164 monthly rebalances, at the cost of a lower return.
  • Picked out the 2011, 2018, 2020 and 2022 stress periods without being told about them.

Python, hmmlearn, PyPortfolioOpt, scikit-learn, pandas, Yahoo Finance data
